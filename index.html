YOUR QUESTIONS

Answers that put safety first.

Scammers often use urgency and secrecy. Trustworthy guidance should stay clear, readable, and verifiable.

Can you guarantee recovery of stolen Bitcoin or crypto?

No. Cryptocurrency transfers are often irreversible, and no legitimate organization can guarantee recovery. We focus on prevention, documentation, safe next steps, and appropriate reporting resources.

Can you help if my Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp was hacked?

Yes, we can help you understand safer next steps and direct you to official recovery routes. We are not the platforms themselves and we do not ask for passwords or verification codes.

Will you ask for my passwords or login codes?

Never. Do not share passwords, one-time verification codes, Social Security numbers, full banking details, crypto private keys, or wallet recovery phrases with us or anyone claiming to help you.

Where can I officially report a scam in the U.S.?

You can report suspected fraud to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov, internet crime to the FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center, and identity theft via IdentityTheft.gov.

Are you part of a government agency or financial institution?

No. Digital Watchdogs is an independent educational initiative. We are not a government agency, law enforcement body, bank, Meta, Google, TikTok, or WhatsApp.
