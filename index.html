YOUR QUESTIONS

Answers that put safety first.

Scammers often use urgency and secrecy. We believe trustworthy guidance should be clear and verifiable.

Can you guarantee recovery of stolen Bitcoin or crypto?

No. Cryptocurrency transfers are often irreversible, and no legitimate organization can guarantee recovery. We focus on prevention, documenting incidents, secure next steps, and directing people to appropriate reporting resources. Watch out for anyone demanding a recovery fee.

Can you help if my Facebook or WhatsApp was hacked?

We can help you understand safety steps and where to find official recovery instructions. For Facebook, start with facebook.com/hacked. For WhatsApp, consult the official WhatsApp Help Center. We are not affiliated with those platforms.

Will you ask for my passwords or login codes?

Never. Do not share passwords, one-time verification codes, Social Security numbers, full banking details, crypto private keys, or wallet recovery phrases with us or with anyone claiming to help you.

Where can I officially report a scam in the U.S.?

You can report suspected fraud to the Federal Trade Commission, internet crime to the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center, and identity theft via IdentityTheft.gov. Contact your bank or payment provider promptly if money is involved.

Are you part of a government agency or financial institution?

No. Digital Watchdogs is an independent educational initiative. We are not a government agency, law enforcement body, bank, Meta, or WhatsApp, and we cannot impersonate or act on behalf of those organizations.
